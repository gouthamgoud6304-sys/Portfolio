R&D to production link

Read new schematics and layouts from the design team, turn them into step-by-step assembly and test checklists and train the assembly technicians on the test procedures.

Bring-up and power profiling

Check the 3.3V and 5V rails for shorts before first power-on on new smart meter prototypes, confirm rail stability under load and measure deep-sleep current in the microamp range.

Pilot batch troubleshooting

Inspect first-article runs for dry solder joints, bridges, reversed capacitors and diodes and misplaced parts. Hand-solder fine-pitch SMD ICs and add jumper-wire fixes to recover faulty boards.

LoRaWAN meter validation

Test gas and electricity meters on STM32WLE5 and Ra-09H modules on the IN865 band: OTAA/ABP joins, spreading factor adaptation, payload format, packet delivery and RSSI/SNR on OrbiWise.

DFM feedback

Keep track of defects that keep coming back from the line and pass them to R&D with suggestions on footprint size, component spacing and test pad locations.

Firmware QA

Develop C++ test routines in PlatformIO (with AI assistance) to stress UART, SPI and I2C buses, log bugs with serial traces and re-test fixes with the firmware engineers.

Also: took part in one flow-testing trial on DN25 ultrasonic water meters at the Fluid Control Research Institute (FCRI), Kerala.

Smart Gas Leak Detector

A gas leak alert node where sensor heat and RF noise were the main reliability problems, so I designed the board layout around them.

  • MQ-5 sensor placed on the opposite side from the ESP32 and the onboard SMA antenna trace, with separate ground, to avoid thermal drift and RF interference.
  • Non-blocking C++ firmware (AI-assisted, tested on hardware) with captive-portal Wi-Fi setup, an SSD1306 OLED and a buzzer.
  • Automatic alerts over Telegram Bot API and SMTP email when the threshold is crossed.

Astronomical Street Light Controller

A lighting controller that replaces LDR sensors, which fail when covered by dirt or bird droppings, with sunrise and sunset times worked out on the ESP32 from location coordinates.

  • Algorithm runs on the ESP32 (AI-assisted code, validated on hardware).
